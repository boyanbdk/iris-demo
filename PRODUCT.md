# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Bulgarian dental-clinic owners and decision-makers evaluating AutoSilas, alongside prospective patients experiencing the fictional clinic as a believable example. Visitors should quickly understand the clinic's care, team, services, and next step while always knowing the site is a demonstration.

## Product Purpose

Show what a modern, trustworthy dental-clinic website built by AutoSilas can feel like. Success means the fictional Iris clinic feels calm, inhabited, and credible; patient-facing information remains easy to scan; and clinic owners can picture the same quality applied to their real practice without mistaking any demo detail for a real claim.

## Brand Personality

Calm, credible, and human. The experience should feel quietly confident and reassuring, with cinematic presence in the hero and disciplined editorial clarity everywhere else.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic clinic templates, glossy luxury advertising, busy media collages, unrelated stock photography, cold science-fiction clinical imagery, and surfaces where every section competes for attention. Never obscure the fictional nature of the clinic or introduce real-world claims, identities, patient information, or contact details.

## Design Principles

- Lead with reassurance: make the clinic feel safe, attentive, and easy to understand.
- Show, then explain: use one decisive hero and coherent fictional photography to establish trust before supporting it with concise content.
- Keep the fiction honest: demo disclosures and invented-data labels remain unmistakable without overwhelming the experience.
- Give every section one job: preserve a clear progression from promise, to capability, to people, to place, to proof, and conversion.
- Use motion sparingly: reserve it for moments that add atmosphere while keeping the page fast and calm.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and keyboard access. Preserve semantic headings, visible focus states, the skip link, meaningful Bulgarian alt text, and layouts that do not depend on hover. Respect reduced-motion preferences by suppressing autoplay and nonessential motion, keep media muted, and maintain readable contrast from 375px mobile through wide desktop screens.
